Privacy Policy

  • Effective 25 October 2026
  • Gloop: Snail Path Puzzle for iPhone and iPad

Gloop is a calm puzzle game. We built it so that your progress belongs to you: there are no accounts, we run no servers of our own, and we never see your game data. Some versions of Gloop show ads and offer optional purchases; in those versions the only third party that receives data from the App is Google, which serves the ads. If your version of the App has no Shop and shows no ads, it sends no data to Google or to any other third party.

The short version

  • Your progress is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud key-value storage. We cannot access either.
  • We do not have user accounts, our own servers or third-party analytics.
  • If your version has no Shop and shows no ads, none of the advertising and purchase parts of this policy apply to you, and no data is collected by Google.
  • In versions that sell items, purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.
  • In versions that show ads, ads are provided by Google AdMob. Ads are only requested after you have made a consent choice where one is required, and your advertising identifier (IDFA) is only available if you allow tracking. You can change your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy Options.
  • You can erase your progress at any time with Settings → Delete my data.

Who we are

Gloop: Snail Path Puzzle (the “App”) is published by Artem Musii (“we”, “us”). For the limited personal data processed in connection with support requests and, in versions that show ads, advertising consent, we are the data controller. You can reach us at artem.musii@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The App saves the following information locally, as a file in the App’s private Application Support folder on your iPhone or iPad:

  • campaign progress, solved levels and best times;
  • Daily puzzle results, streaks and calendar history;
  • your Drops balance, hint count and unlocked or equipped cosmetics;
  • your settings, such as sound, haptics and display preferences.

The App also records simple usage events (for example, “level completed”) with Apple’s on-device logging system so we can debug the App during development. These logs stay on your device and are not sent to us or to any analytics provider. This data never leaves your device unless you use iCloud sync as described below.

iCloud key-value sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the App keeps a copy of your progress in your personal iCloud key-value storage so it can stay in sync across your iPhone and iPad. This data is stored by Apple in your own iCloud account under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We have no access to it. If you are not signed in to iCloud, your progress simply stays on the device.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the App submits your Daily puzzle times to a leaderboard and reports achievements. Game Center is operated by Apple; your Game Center nickname and scores may be visible to other players according to your Game Center settings. Using Game Center is optional and the game is fully playable without it. We do not receive your Apple ID or contact details through Game Center.

Advertising and purchases (only in versions that show ads or sell items)

Some versions of Gloop are supported by ads and offer optional in-app purchases and a subscription. Everything in this section applies only to those versions.

Does this apply to you? If the App you have contains no Shop and shows no ads, none of this section applies. That version contains no advertising software, asks for no ad consent and no tracking permission, offers nothing for sale, and no data is collected by Google.

Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never receive your name, payment card or billing details. The App uses StoreKit 2 to verify your purchases on the device and to unlock what you bought. Apple shares sales reports with us that do not identify you personally.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Versions of the App that show ads are supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). In those versions, the Google Mobile Ads SDK is the only third-party software in the App that sends data off your device. According to Google’s own disclosures, it may collect:

  • Device identifiers — the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) only if you allow tracking through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, and other device or app-scoped identifiers used for things such as frequency capping and fraud prevention;
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (not precise GPS location);
  • Usage and advertising data, such as app launches, ad impressions, taps and video views;
  • Diagnostic data, such as SDK crash logs and performance metrics;
  • Device information, such as model, operating system version, language and the App’s version.

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you have given consent, to personalise ads. If you do not consent or do not allow tracking, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads. You can read more in Google’s Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

How ads appear in versions that show them: rewarded videos are always optional; interstitial ads only appear from level 12, and never during onboarding or after the Daily puzzle. There are no banner ads. Buying “No Ads” removes interstitial ads, and Gloop+ removes all ads.

Consent (Google UMP)

In versions that show ads, if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region where consent is required, the App shows a consent form provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) the first time you open it. No ads are requested until you have made your choice. Your choice is stored on your device by the UMP SDK.

You can review or change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy Options, which reopens the consent form.

App Tracking Transparency

Apple requires apps to ask your permission before your data is used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. Versions of Gloop that show ads ask only after the consent step above and after you have completed level 3, and it first shows its own short screen explaining what the permission means. You are free to decline: the game works exactly the same, with the same levels, rewards and features. Versions without ads never show this prompt.

You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. In versions that show ads, we configure AdMob to serve only ads with a maximum content rating of G (suitable for general audiences). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at artem.musii@gmail.com and we will help you with the steps described in this policy.

Retention and deletion

  • In the App: Settings → Delete my data resets your local progress and removes the App’s data from your iCloud key-value storage.
  • On your device: deleting the App removes its local files. Data kept in iCloud stays in your account until you use Delete my data.
  • Purchases (only in versions that sell items): non-consumable purchases and subscriptions are linked to your Apple ID and can be restored with Restore Purchases. Consumed items, such as a Drops balance, cannot be restored after a reset.
  • Game Center: scores and achievements are managed by Apple in your Game Center account.
  • Google (only in versions that show ads): data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is retained according to Google’s policies. You can manage it through Google’s privacy controls, reset your advertising choices in Privacy Options, or contact us at artem.musii@gmail.com and we will help you with your request.

Because we do not run servers, we hold no copy of your game data that we could delete or hand over. Support emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

Your rights (GDPR and CCPA)

EEA, UK and Switzerland

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. In versions that show ads, personalised advertising and tracking rely on your consent, and non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and ad measurement rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free game. In versions that sell items, providing purchases relies on performing our contract with you. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California and other US states

We do not sell personal information for money. In versions that show ads, allowing personalised ads may involve Google receiving identifiers and usage data, which some US state laws treat as “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out by declining the tracking prompt, by choosing your preferences in Settings → Privacy Options where available, or by enabling iOS tracking restrictions. You have the right to know, delete and correct personal information and you will not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

To exercise any right, contact artem.musii@gmail.com. As we do not hold your game data, most requests are fulfilled directly in the App or through Apple and, where it applies, Google, and we will guide you.

No servers of our own

Gloop has no login and does not connect to any server operated by us. We do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services. Apart from Apple services you choose to use (iCloud, Game Center, App Store) and, in versions that show ads, the Google advertising SDKs described above, the App does not transmit your data.

International transfers and security

Apple and, in versions that show ads, Google may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. Data stored on your device is protected by iOS security features. iCloud data is protected by Apple.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or the law changes. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date, and for significant changes we will also let you know in the App or in the release notes.
