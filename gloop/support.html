Support

Stuck on a level or a setting? Here are answers to the most common questions. If you still need help, write to us at artem.musii@gmail.com.

Playing

How do I play?

Draw one continuous line, Gloop’s slime trail, that runs through every cell of the grid.

  • Pass the numbered cells strictly in order: 1 → 2 → 3 … up to the last number.
  • Move only horizontally or vertically, never diagonally.
  • From level 40 some cells have walls between them. The trail cannot cross a wall.
  • The level is solved when the trail has covered every cell with all numbers passed in order.

There is no timer pressure and there are no lives. The stopwatch is just for your information.

How do hints work?

A hint draws the next 3 correct steps of the trail. You start with 3 free hints and receive 1 more free hint each day you open the game. When you run out, a hint costs 40 Drops. If your version offers ads, you can also watch an optional rewarded video to get one, and if it offers Gloop+, members have unlimited hints.

What are Drops?

Drops are Gloop’s slime-drop currency. You earn them by playing. If your version has a Shop, you can also optionally buy packs there. Spend them on shells and trail colours in the Collection, or on hints. You also get a free shell every 50 campaign levels. Drops have no cash value; see our Terms of Use.

When does the Daily puzzle change?

A new 7×7 Daily puzzle starts every day at 00:00 UTC, and it is the same puzzle for everyone. Solve it each day to grow your streak. If you are signed in to Game Center, your time is submitted to the Daily leaderboard. Past Daily puzzles can be replayed from the Archive. In versions without a Shop the Archive is open to everyone; in versions that offer Gloop+, it is a Gloop+ perk.

Can I turn off sounds or haptics?

Yes. Both can be switched off separately in Settings. The App also follows your system settings for Dark Mode, text size (Dynamic Type), VoiceOver and Reduce Motion.

Purchases and ads (only in versions that offer them)

If your version of Gloop has no Shop and shows no ads, it is completely free and this section does not apply: there is nothing to buy, restore or cancel, and no ads are ever shown.

How do I restore my purchases?

If your version offers purchases, make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy, then open Settings → Restore Purchases (also available on the Gloop+ screen). “No Ads” and an active Gloop+ subscription will be restored. Drops are consumable, so they cannot be restored through the App Store, but your balance syncs with your progress through iCloud.

How do I cancel my Gloop+ subscription?

If your version offers Gloop+: subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Gloop+ and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also get there from Settings → Manage Subscription in the game. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (or of the free trial) to avoid being charged for the next one. You keep Gloop+ until the paid period ends. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

When do ads appear?

If your version shows ads, these rules apply. Versions without ads never show any.

  • Rewarded videos are always optional: you choose to watch one for a hint or to double a level reward.
  • Interstitial ads only appear from level 12, at most after every 3rd completed level, at least 120 seconds apart, never in the first 2 minutes of a session, never during onboarding and never after the Daily puzzle.
  • There are no banner ads.

The one-time “No Ads” purchase removes interstitial ads forever. Gloop+ removes all ads.

I bought something but did not receive it

If your version offers purchases, first try Settings → Restore Purchases and make sure you have an internet connection. If the item is still missing, email artem.musii@gmail.com with the date of purchase and the item name. For refunds, please contact Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, as we cannot issue refunds ourselves.

Progress, data and privacy

How do I sync progress across my devices?

Gloop syncs automatically through your own iCloud. Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple ID on your iPhone and iPad and your progress, Drops and collection will follow you. There is no separate account to create. If you are not signed in to iCloud, progress stays on the device where you play.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Delete my data. This resets your local progress and removes Gloop’s data from your iCloud key-value storage. This cannot be undone, and your Drops balance will be lost. If your version offers purchases, those tied to your Apple ID (“No Ads” and Gloop+) can be restored afterwards. For data held by Google for advertising (only in versions that show ads), see our Privacy Policy or contact artem.musii@gmail.com.

How do I change my ad consent or privacy choices?

If your version shows ads, open Settings → Privacy Options in the game to reopen the consent form and change your choices at any time. Versions without ads ask for no ad consent and never show a tracking prompt. To change whether apps may ask to track you, use iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining tracking or personalised ads never affects gameplay.

Do I need Game Center?

No. Game Center is optional and only used for leaderboards and achievements. Everything else works without it.
